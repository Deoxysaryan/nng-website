# NNG website prototype: design audit, 23 September 2026

Scope: the five pages of `clients/nng-website` after the second edition (template patterns removed, background drawings added). Aryan asked for the design's inefficiencies and visual issues to be audited and all of them fixed, with every image checked, then a public link for review. This file records what was measured, what was found, what changed, and what was left as it is.

## How it was measured

- Playwright (Chromium) on the static export served from `out/`: computed styles for every rule in question, every `img`, `svg` and CSS background image on every page at 1440 and 390 px (on the local build, the public preview and the portable package), 12 screen sizes from 320 × 568 to 1920 × 1080 including a landscape phone and two iPads, the phone behaviours (16 checks) and the interactions (19 checks).
- axe-core (WCAG 2.1 AA) on every page at both widths.
- Lighthouse 12, mobile, simulated slow 4G with 4x CPU slowdown, on the export served with gzip (`scripts/serve-out.mjs`), three runs per page, median reported.
- A dead-selector probe (`querySelector` for every selector in the stylesheet across all five pages, dialog and menu open) instead of Chrome's CSS coverage, which mis-attributed rules.
- Full-page snapshots before and after each change, compared pixel for pixel, so only the intended regions differ.

## Findings and fixes

P1 (fix before release)
- Side stripes as accents: `border-left: 2px solid gold` on the method quote, the identity lock-up, her Services quotes and the dialog's message preview. Replaced by a 32 px gold hairline above quotes and the lock-up (`::before`) and a rose-pale block for the preview.
- Moving content with no pause: the graha orbit turns continuously. `OrbitControl.tsx` adds a pause and play button beside the portrait (44 px, `aria-pressed`, remembered for the session, hidden in the PDF export); `html.orbit-paused` pauses the animation. It sits outside the arch on wide screens and in the empty corner above the arch below 1190 px, inside the page edge on every device.
- Backdrops behind text on phones: the kundli and vastu drawings sat under paragraphs at 390 px. Off below 680 px.
- Caption at 10 px: the wine panel's "months of personal guidance" on phones. Now 12.5 px, no letter spacing.
- Tiles overflowing at 320 px: the four-drawing grid's columns could not shrink (`1fr` keeps min-content). `minmax(0, 1fr)` and `min-width: 0`.

P2 (fix in this pass)
- Hard-coded colours: `#000` (two), `#fff`, `rgb(0 0 0 / .5)`, `#9f2f2d`. Tokens `--night`, `--paper`, `--error`.
- Layout animation: `.section-chips` transitioned `top`. Now `--chips-top` and a `transform` when the header hides.
- Cascade fault: the About book quote was 15 px on desktop and 22 px on phones because `.book-feature p` came after `.method-quote p`. Explicit rule; 24 px, 22 px on phones like the other quotes.
- Dead stylesheet: 47 rules no page used (eyebrows, the old service index, hero stats, portrait variants) and an appended override block. Removed and folded into the originals; `globals.css` 3981 lines to 3743, 11.3 KB gzipped; `.eyebrow` renamed `.kicker`.
- Repetition: the same four clients filmed on the homepage and the program page (now Navleen, Shelley, Deepa and Shashi at home; Renu and Shelley on the program page); the Mind Training lead repeated a FAQ answer; the dialog heading was italic. Film captions lead with the film's title.
- Logo served as one 800 px PNG at 94 px: WebP at 200, 400 and 800 px with `srcset`. An unused portrait file removed (12 image files, 615 KB, in `public/images`).
- Phone density: the four-drawing grid took most of a screen (tiles now 1.3:1) and the "How a consultation works" steps were loose (gap 18 px, heading 19 px).
- The dialog's "Your message" label was styled apart from the other labels; now the same 12 px 600 label.

- Review banner outside landmarks: `PreviewNotice` rendered a `div role="note"` above the header, so axe's best-practice `region` rule failed on every page of a preview build. It is `role="region"` with the name "Review notice".
- Route prefetch on a static host: every `Link` in view asked the server for the router payload of its route, and a static host answers with the HTML page, so Next abandoned each request (`net::ERR_ABORTED`): five wasted requests on every page view, one per navigation link. `prefetch={false}` on all eleven links; navigation itself is unchanged (a click still loads the next page in full, as it did).

P3 (noted)
- Drive as the image host: client faces and film posters come from `lh3.googleusercontent.com`. After several hundred automated requests from this Mac in one evening the host began answering 429 (an HTML page), which Chrome then blocks for an `img` (`ERR_BLOCKED_BY_ORB`), so a face or poster can go missing for a reviewer who reloads many times. Every image check earlier in the evening loaded 0 broken across the build, the public preview and the package. The launch list already moves the films and their posters off Drive; until then this is the one thing on the preview that can fail on its own.
- Fonts are WOFF; WOFF2 would save roughly 30 percent but needs `fonttools` and `brotli`, which are not installed and would need a download. Left.
- The framework ships 186 KB of gzipped JavaScript for five interactive parts. A plain build would be lighter, but the designer reproduces the components, not the framework. Left.
- Playfair Display and DM Sans are common pairings. They are Aryan's approved choice. Left.

## Results after the fixes

- axe: 0 violations, five pages, 1440 and 390 px, build and package, with the WCAG 2.0, 2.1 and 2.2 AA rules and the best-practice rules. The one best-practice finding on the way (the review banner outside every landmark, `region`, on every page of a preview build) is fixed: the banner is a named region.
- Devices: 72 page and dialog views, 0 issues (no sideways scroll, no text under 10 px, no tap target under 44 px, every dialog fits).
- Phone behaviours 16 of 16; interactions 19 of 19; portable package 36 of 36 and pixel-identical to the build at both widths.
- Prefetch: after the change, a page load and a hover over the navigation make no route requests; a click makes one request for the route's static payload and the page changes with the tilak transition, as before.
- Lighthouse mobile (median of three, gzip): Performance Home 94, Services 99, Program 98, About 92, Contact 97; Accessibility 100 and Best Practices 100 on every page; LCP 2.1 to 3.3 s, CLS 0, total blocking time under 17 ms. SEO reads 66 to 69 only because the prototype is `noindex`. Measured on the build without the review banner, served with gzip; served uncompressed the same pages score 79 to 82.

## Rules that carry forward

Recorded in `.impeccable.md` (principle 11) and the handoff: every colour a token; no side stripe over 1 px; motion by transform and opacity only, with a pause for anything continuous; backdrops off phones where text would sit on them; every image checked on every page before a build goes out; the stylesheet kept to what the pages use.
