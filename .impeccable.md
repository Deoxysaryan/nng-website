## Design Context

### Users
Sanatani, educated Indians and NRIs, mostly 35 and over, business-literate (owners and managers a level below CEO), on mid-range Android phones, comfortable in Hindi and English. They have already tried remedies and are still stuck. They believe in the shastra and refuse to be frightened by it. They arrive from YouTube, Instagram and WhatsApp shares, often in the evening, tired and a little sceptical. The job: decide within a minute whether she is credible and whether this is for them, then start one conversation.

### Brand Personality
Rooted, exact, unhurried. Calm confidence, trust without hype, and the relief of someone finally looking at the real thing. Physical reference: a printed almanac and an account book on warm paper, in red-brown ink.

### Aesthetic Direction
Updated 23 Sept 2026: Aryan chose the Codex homepage design for every page (the 21 Sept system of Tiro Devanagari Hindi, Hind and a rosewood accent is archived in `_archive/v2-homepage-2026-09-21`).

Light theme on warm ivory paper (#faf7f1) with ink (#302824), one wine accent (#754947) for buttons, bands and italic accent words, rose (#e8dbd4) for text on wine, rose pale (#f1eae4) for alternate bands, and gold (#a58a60) only for the arch outline and the quote rule. Corners 3 px, 1 px hairlines. Playfair Display (regular and italic) for headings, figures and quotes; DM Sans (400 and 600) for everything else; Devanagari falls back to Tiro Devanagari Hindi and Hind. Arch portrait with her figures beside it, a wine ribbon of Health, Relationship, Career and Money, quotes and films as swipe rows, the tilak on paper as the page transition.

Anti-references: AI composite imagery, zodiac wheels and crimson gradient logos, generic astrology sites (purple, cosmic, om, crystals), AI landing page tropes (gradients, glass, icon-card grids, gradient text), urgency lines and floating chat bubbles.

### Design Principles
1. One door and one filled action per view. Every button leads to the same conversation.
2. Real over decorated. Real photographs where they exist and original drawn diagrams where they do not, never AI imagery. Verified numbers (21+ years, 1,200+ clients, 4+ countries, confirmed 23 Sept 2026), client words only with consent, and no prices until January 2027.
3. Colour is scarce: paper, ink, one wine accent; gold only for the arch and the quote rule.
4. Type carries the personality: large, calm and readable for 35 and over on a phone.
5. Motion is quiet and comes from meaning. The graha orbit turns once every four minutes with a pause button; since 24 Sept 2026 every drawing draws itself in on first view and keeps one slow motion from what it depicts (the mind's threads gather, the Lo Shu line finds each fifteen, the compass swings), the band orbits turn once every eight minutes, and the contact drawings answer a hover. Nothing bounces, nothing loops fast, and all of it stops for reduced motion and in print. Buttons press down onto a gold plate rather than lifting.
6. No claim without a source. No fear. No urgency.
7. Visual first, words last. One short line per idea. Every section carries a photograph, a drawing or a diagram. Before showing a page, measure words per 1,000 px and the share of the page that is media against the reference, at 390 px wide, where about 95 percent of visits happen.
8. Phone first: the header steps away while reading and returns on the way up, one bar that waits for the first button to scroll off, sheets from the bottom, films full screen. No pop-ups, chat bubbles or buttons floating over text.
9. Nothing that reads as a template: no labels above headings, no italic accent words in headings (italic is for spoken words and book titles), no arrows on internal links, numbers only where order matters, real faces instead of initials, whole specific sentences instead of two-beat fragments, and a source line under figures.
10. Background drawings come from her practice, never from generic astrology art: the nine grahas and their numbers, the Lo Shu square in its true order, a home's plan for vastu, the North Indian chart, and for the mind five threads gathering into one line. Hairline ink, faint, never over text.
11. Nothing hard-coded and nothing that only looks like design (audit of 23 Sept 2026): every colour is a token; no side stripe thicker than 1 px (a quote or lock-up gets a short gold hairline above it, a note gets a rose-pale block); motion only by transform and opacity, and anything that keeps moving has a pause control; faint backdrops stay off phones where they would sit behind text; every image is checked on every page before a build goes out.
